## Overview

**BurndownBar** — *May you not burndown your AI tokens.*

A lightweight, native macOS menu-bar app for tracking AI coding subscriptions, token usage, prompt cache reads, and inference spend in one place — built in pure SwiftUI and AppKit with zero external dependencies.

## Highlights

- **Unified Menu-Bar Monitor**: Displays the lowest remaining quota percentage across enabled accounts right in the macOS menu bar, organized across **Subscriptions**, **Inference**, **Rankings**, and **Model Advisor**.
- **Token, Cache & Cost Charts**: 7-day and 30-day charts breaking down input, output, cache read, and cache write tokens alongside USD spend.
- **Pace Markers & Countdown Timers**: Linear pace indicators comparing remaining allowance against time left in `5-hour`, `Daily`, `Weekly`, and `Monthly` windows.
- **OpenRouter Dual-Key Intelligence**: Credit runway estimates, 30-day cache analytics, per-key spend attribution, and inline guardrail controls.
- **Zero External Packages**: Compiles directly with Apple's Swift toolchain against `AppKit`, `SwiftUI`, `Charts`, `Security`, `LocalAuthentication`, and `sqlite3`.

## Supported Providers

Tracks quotas, usage, and spend across **OpenRouter**, **Codex**, **Cursor**, **Droid / Factory**, **OpenCode**, **Antigravity**, **Copilot**, **Warp**, **Zed**, **Claude**, and **Grok**.
